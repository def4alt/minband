# Older links settled what MinBand rediscovers

Drone warfare's link problems are not new, and the fields that met them first left rules MinBand can copy in hours rather than invent on stage. The three strongest cross-domain findings are these. First, the control-theory result behind the whole design: under a deterministic prediction-error trigger a non-update *is* information, but only while loss is excluded, which means MinBand's keyframe is not just a repair mechanism, it is what keeps "silence = within threshold" true, and a missed keyframe must change what the operator sees, not merely an error bound ([Trimpe & D'Andrea 2012](https://is.mpg.de/uploads/publication_attachment/attachment/320/trimpe-CDC12.pdf); [FAA AD 2017-22-14](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2017-11-15/pdf/2017-24066.pdf)). Second, every mature state broadcast carries its own per-message error bar: ADS-B's NIC/NACp/SIL tables as implemented in pyModeS 3.6.0, MAVLink `HIGH_LATENCY2`'s one-byte "maximum error since last message" as implemented in pymavlink 2.4.50, Link 16's 4-bit Track Quality because a full covariance "takes 21 real numbers" the link cannot afford ([US 8052054](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8052054)); MinBand sends detector confidence but never its own active threshold, so the twin cannot draw an honest uncertainty ring or fill CoT `ce`/`le`. Third, on LoRa and mesh the budget is airtime, not bytes: a 16-byte LongFast packet costs 354 ms ([D-Central](https://d-central.tech/lora-airtime-calculator/)), so MinBand's 60 B/s static-scene floor would occupy roughly 70 % of a LongFast channel by itself, and Silvus's 559-node demo spent under 35 % of airtime on position reports alone ([UST](https://www.unmannedsystemstechnology.com/2022/10/large-scale-tactical-mesh-radio-network-demonstrated/)). The warfare evidence says MinBand fits the reconnaissance hand-off, C2 picture, relay-mesh and BLOS-fallback roles, and does not fit terminal guidance, battle-damage proof or UGV teleoperation, where pixels are the currency by doctrine and incentive. For the stretch list this means: move S1 (simplex), S6 (compact codec), S8 (image chip) and S13 (utility test) up; change S4 and S5; drop S11 for the weekend; add a one-byte threshold field, a coasting state, keyframe pacing and a receiver plausibility gate as new items. Nothing here displaces the P0 demo; the first four new items together are under a day of work and each strengthens a visual already planned. Every note behind this report was snippet-based because page fetches were proxy-blocked, so the numbers below carry their verification tags and the team should quote the [V] ones on stage.

## Evidence quality: snippets, source-read implementations, and what the tags mean

All seven notes were written without working page fetches; findings rest on search-result snippets of the cited pages, and the cross-domain researcher instead downloaded open-source implementations of the relevant standards from PyPI and read their source. This report carries those tags through. **[V]** means verified this session from an implementation's source and is quoted here as "as implemented in `<package> <version>`"; it is evidence of what the standard encodes, not of the standard's text. **[U]** means an unverified recollection of a standard, paper or talk; where the note supplied a canonical URL it is cited, where it supplied none the item is marked "unsourced" and is kept out of headline numbers. The warfare, Korea and red-team numbers are "as reported in snippet" and are mostly self-reported by combatants, vendors or regulators; where two sources conflict both are shown. The practical rule for 11 October: a judge can be given a [V] number or a sourced warfare number without hedging; a [U] number should be prefaced with "as I recall from the standard, to be checked"; an unsourced [U] should not be said at all.

## Which warfare challenges actually call for a MinBand-class link

The obvious findings take one line each: digital FPV video needs 12–50 Mbit/s ([uavmodel](https://blog.uavmodel.com/walksnail-vs-dji-o4-vs-hdzero-digital-fpv-system-comparison-2026/)), EW downs 31 % to 75 % of FPV sorties depending on who counts ([War on the Rocks](https://warontherocks.com/i-fought-in-ukraine-and-heres-why-fpv-drones-kind-of-suck/); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2025/01/14/why-fpv-drones-are-still-ukraines-biggest-tank-killers/)), and fibre-optic drones are at most ~15 % of the line of contact with a 10 km workhorse spool ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/articles/2026/01/25/8017810/); [TWZ](https://www.twz.com/news-features/inside-ukraines-fiber-optic-drone-war)). The less obvious shape of the problem is that the dominant failure near the target is **binary denial, not graceful degradation**, and that the fielded answers are physical (fibre, no-RF loitering, CRPA antennas) or computational (on-board terminal guidance, optical re-localisation), not better telemetry. Bandwidth efficiency is a second-order concern in the cited material, which is why MinBand's honest pitch is "the picture survives the link dying", not "we replace video".

Where a predictor-based, loss-tolerant, low-bandwidth state link is genuinely needed, the evidence is specific. Ground stations get found: operators in Ukraine have been targeted by artillery after their drone signals were geolocated ([CRFS](https://www.crfs.com/blog/emissions-control-emcon-for-ground-forces)), Ukraine fields a balloon-borne SIGINT system to locate Russian operators ([TWZ](https://www.twz.com/air/balloon-based-sensor-that-pinpoints-location-of-drone-operators-emerges-in-ukraine)), and direction-finding is commoditised down to a paid TinySA guide ([Cy-Hawk Labs](https://cyhawklabs.gumroad.com/l/upgfz)); today MinBand's ground station acks up to ten times a second (`server/src/world.ts`, `ACK_INTERVAL_MS = 100`). Relay chains are airtime-bound: Russian Shahed/Gerbera mesh chains are kept to "no more than two or three retransmissions" because each hop costs speed and latency ([Hvylya](https://amp.en.hvylya.net/news/2414-shahed-drones-with-mesh-modems-reach-kyiv-and-odesa-flash-maps-the-strike-zone)), relay drones hover at 2–3 km for hours as continuous emitters ([Ukraine's Arms Monitor](https://ukrainesarmsmonitor.substack.com/p/drone-warfare-in-ukraine-military)), and 3 % of one unit's missions failed from interference by other drones ([War on the Rocks](https://warontherocks.com/i-fought-in-ukraine-and-heres-why-fpv-drones-kind-of-suck/)). BLOS fallbacks are kilobits or worse: STANAG 4539 gives 3,200–9,600 bit/s per 3 kHz HF channel ([sigidwiki](https://sigidwiki.com/wiki/STANAG_4539)), Iridium SBD is a 340-byte message every 10–15 s ([PX4](https://docs.px4.io/v1.14/en/advanced_features/satcom_roadblock.html); [RockBLOCK](https://www.unmannedsystemstechnology.com/company/ground-control/rockblock-9603/)), and an ArduPilot test over Iridium Certus 100 measured 0.8 s latency with 7–8 % UDP loss and halved the MAVLink rate to cope ([ArduPilot forum](https://discuss.ardupilot.org/t/ardupilot-and-the-iridium-certus-satellite-service/142601)), which is manual threshold widening. The link that drones already carry is smaller still: ExpressLRS telemetry at the default ratio is on the order of **78 bit/s** ([ExpressLRS](https://www.expresslrs.org/info/telem-bandwidth/)), so even MinBand's `telemetry` profile at 600 bit/s is generous against the radio it is named for.

Nation-scale sensor networks already run the shape MinBand proposes. Ukraine's Sky Fortress is **9,500 to ~14,000 Android-phone acoustic sensors** on cellular backhaul feeding the Virazh C2, with roughly 20 % of targets detected only acoustically ([Janes](https://www.janes.com/defence-intelligence-insights/defence-news/weapons/ukraine-conflict-ukrainian-air-defence-employs-passive-sensors-for-detection-and-tracking); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/5/4/what-is-the-ukrainian-anti-drone-system-sky-map-being-used-in-the-gulf)); each node contributes a bearing and a time, and the hard problem is fusing and extrapolating tracks between sparse detections, which is dead reckoning. On orbit, Ubotica claims a **200-byte ship-detection message in place of a 3 GB image** ([Ubotica](https://ubotica.com/press-release-cognisat-6-breakthrough/)) because a LEO satellite is in contact with a given station for under 2 % of the day ([SpaceNexus](https://spacenexus.us/learn/communications/space-communications-101/ground-stations-and-latency)). Ukraine's Delta cut detection-to-strike from ~20 minutes to 3–5 ([RBC-Ukraine](https://newsukraine.rbc.ua/news/ukraine-has-battlefield-edge-us-is-now-trying-1787815768.html)), and the recognised failure in that chain is "a stale coordinate, weak identification, or poor handoff" ([MWI](https://mwi.westpoint.edu/borrow-what-works-the-case-for-army-adoption-of-ukraines-combat-tested-horizontal-kill-web-architecture/)); a dead-reckoned entity with a visible age is the direct answer to a stale coordinate. Project Convergence, the US Army's flagship experiment, "ran on a network that was essentially pristine" ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/land/2024/10/16/how-an-experiment-in-new-jersey-could-shape-the-armys-future-network/)), which is the strongest available justification for a degraded-network demo.

### Silence is only information when loss is ruled out

The deepest result in the notes is also the one MinBand's design already half-encodes. In event-based state estimation the sensor "runs a copy of the remote estimator and transmits a measurement if the associated measurement prediction variance exceeds a tolerable threshold" ([Trimpe & D'Andrea, CDC 2012](https://is.mpg.de/uploads/publication_attachment/attachment/320/trimpe-CDC12.pdf); [IEEE TAC 2014](https://al.is.mpg.de/uploads_file/attachment/attachment/322/trimpe-TAC14b.pdf)), which is the ghost in `core/src/edge.rs` with variance in place of position. The send-on-delta Kalman literature adds that the filter "treats silence as information" ([Automatica 2007](https://bibsonomy.org/bibtex/1d01439c1a21fccabf8f394f63142874)), and the delta can be chosen in real time for a desired mean transmission rate, which "improves deeply the tracking-error while maintaining the desired average throughput" ([Diaz-Cacho et al., Sensors 2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5335925/)); that is the budget controller with a 2017 citation. The unverified but central corollary [U] is that with packet loss silence becomes ambiguous and periodic "time-synchronous" updates have to be added. For MinBand this reframes the 2 s keyframe: in simulation a heartbeat is bookkeeping, on a lossy link it is the only evidence the edge is alive and the predictor is still being checked. Two consequences follow. The receiver can tighten the entity's uncertainty on every silent tick while the device's acks are recent, because the deterministic trigger guarantees the ghost error is inside θ, and this is a free accuracy gain the twin currently throws away. And the receiver must treat one missed keyframe period as a state change, not wait for `stale_ticks` at 6 s; today the viewer's decay schedule (`SOLID_S = 3`, `OUTLINE_S = 8` in `viewer/src/scene.ts`) starts counting only after that 6 s. Reported savings from event-triggered schemes range from 17 % to 86 % at 16 % accuracy loss on a testbed ([Alanwar et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.00493)), so the 86 % figure is an upper bound to cite, not a prediction; MinBand's own synthetic 11–65× against naive metadata is the number to defend.

The accident record puts a price on getting this wrong. A 2017 FAA airworthiness directive was prompted by "five instances of air traffic control observing coasting (extrapolated stale data)" where "the extrapolation of the data occurs with no warning to the crew" ([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2017-11-15/pdf/2017-24066.pdf)). In a Blue Force Tracker experiment, adding a **10-second latency** to position updates made participants "significantly more likely to engage friendly targets" ([PubMed 23516795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23516795/)), and fielded BFT "can have a five-minute latency for ground assets" ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/258014391_Blue_Force_Tracking_Effects_of_Spatial_Error_on_Soldier_Performance)). Patriot's 2003 fratricides were traced to operators "trained to trust the system's software" ([SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/?p=7463)). The mature conventions all push the same way: ADS-B decoders keep a last accepted position as a local reference for 30 s and evict an aircraft after 300 s silent, as implemented in pyModeS 3.6.0 (`_pipe.py`) ([pyModeS](https://pypi.org/project/pyModeS/3.6.0/)); MAVLink's `ADSB_VEHICLE` carries `tslc`, time since last communication in seconds, as implemented in pymavlink 2.4.50 ([pymavlink](https://pypi.org/project/pymavlink/2.4.50/)); Eurocontrol ASTERIX CAT062 has a CST "coasted" bit and per-sensor track ages in 0.25 s units [U]; the Source engine caps extrapolation at 0.25 s and freezes rather than keeps flying [U, [Valve](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Source_Multiplayer_Networking)]; AIS's time-stamp field reserves value 62 for "position is dead-reckoned" [U]; and the WWII filter room coloured every plot by a five-minute sector clock so age was visible at a glance [U, unsourced]. Drone warfare is rediscovering all of this through Delta's "stale coordinate". The team's claim should therefore be "degrades visibly through blackouts", with V2's rings as the proof, and the Q&A answer to "what does the operator see at t+5 s, t+30 s, t+5 min" should be rehearsed with exact states.

### Every mature state broadcast carries its own error bar, and MinBand's does not

ADS-B packs lat/lon into 17 bits each by Compact Position Reporting, decodable only because the receiver is assumed to hold recent state: a global decode needs an even and an odd frame ≤ 10 s apart, a local decode a reference within 180 NM airborne or 45 NM on the surface, as implemented in pyModeS 3.6.0 (`_cpr.py`) ([pyModeS](https://pypi.org/project/pyModeS/3.6.0/)). Every position then carries self-declared categories: NIC 11 means a 7.5 m containment radius, NACp 11 a 3 m estimated position uncertainty, NACv 4 a 0.3 m/s velocity error, and SIL 3 a 10⁻⁷ probability of exceeding the radius, all in a few bits ([pyModeS](https://pypi.org/project/pyModeS/3.6.0/), `_uncertainty.py`). MAVLink's `HIGH_LATENCY2`, "appropriate for high latency connections like Iridium", fits a full vehicle status in **42 bytes** with heading in 2° units, speeds in 0.2 m/s units and `eph`/`epv` as "maximum error … since last message", as implemented in pymavlink 2.4.50 ([pymavlink](https://pypi.org/project/pymavlink/2.4.50/)). Link 16's 4-bit Track Quality exists precisely because "error covariance data takes 21 real numbers" and the link only allows full covariance on request ([US 8052054](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8052054)). Argos attaches a location class (3: < 250 m … 0: > 1,500 m) to every wildlife fix [U], and ETSI's CAM pairs every field with a sentinel and a confidence [U].

MinBand's `EntityState` carries `conf: u8`, which is the detector's confidence, and nothing about sync accuracy; `theta_scale` lives in `EdgeStats` on the phone and never crosses the wire. The twin therefore cannot know whether a silent entity is within 0.15 m or within 1.95 m of truth (the controller's clamp, `theta_scale_min 0.33 … max 13.0` in `edge.rs`), and the P1 CoT exporter has no honest value for `ce`. One byte per Delta and Keyframe fixes this, and the precedents say one byte is the right size. The same byte is what V2's ring radius should come from, and it is what lets a ROK or NATO consumer discount a track correctly. This is the single most valuable protocol change the weekend can afford, and it is small enough to regenerate the golden vectors for.

### Packing and cadence: the tiers other fields defined

The field accepted different quantisation per link class long ago. AIS's Class A position report is exactly 168 bits with lat/lon at 1/10,000 arc-minute (≈ 0.185 m), while its long-range satellite message 27 is **96 bits** at 1/10 arc-minute (≈ 185 m) with 6-bit speed and 9-bit course, as implemented in pyais 3.3.0 (`messages.py`) ([pyais](https://pypi.org/project/pyais/3.3.0/)). The lesson for S6 is that the compact tier is a different message with coarser semantics, not the same message packed tighter, and that a tile-relative encoding needs the CPR-style rule for how stale the tile reference may be. MAVLink 2 also truncates trailing zero bytes from the payload, as implemented in pymavlink 2.4.50 (`mavutil.py`), a free trick for sparse deltas where zero means unchanged. Varints and zig-zag encoding exist as implemented in protobuf 7.36.2 ([protobuf](https://pypi.org/project/protobuf/7.36.2/)), which `postcard` already uses; the next step down is bit packing. The byte ladder for one position report, from the contested-links note, runs ADS-B 14 B (7 B payload) < Remote ID 25 B < Link 16 one standard slot ≈ 26 B of data < Meshtastic TAKPacketV2 **median 87 B, max 184 B** within a 237 B MTU ([TAKPacket SDK](https://klibs.io/project/meshtastic/TAKPacket-SDK)) < the older atak-forwarder PLI ~190 B ([atak-forwarder](https://github.com/paulmandal/atak-forwarder)) < CoT XML 400–2,300 B. MinBand's 8–16 B target sits at the ADS-B end and is defensible only because it carries no callsign or uid strings, which should be said. The decisive Meshtastic detail is that its compressor "returns nothing if it still does not fit" the 225-byte budget ([Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org/docs/software/apple/developer/tak-protocol/)): existing low-bandwidth CoT paths drop rather than degrade, which is exactly the gap the budget controller fills.

Cadence is also a link-class parameter elsewhere. DIS's heartbeat is 5 s with thresholds described as 1 m and 3° ([patent 8834163](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8834163); [arXiv 1008.3758](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.3758)), AIS reports every 2 s when fast or turning and every 3 min at anchor [U], Unreal keeps a 2 Hz floor for unchanged actors [U], and over Iridium SBD a 2 s keyframe is physically impossible, so the period must stretch to about 15 s to fit one 340 B message per round trip. MinBand's `keyframe_ticks`, `hello_refresh_ticks` and the iOS `poseInterval` (0.5 s in `Pipeline.swift`) are all constants; the P0 budget fix already derives the pose interval from the budget, and the same derivation should cover the keyframe period. ETSI's CAM rule adds the anti-flapping piece: generate on Δheading ≥ 4°, Δposition > 4 m or Δspeed > 0.5 m/s, never faster than `T_GenCamMin` = 100 ms nor slower than 1 s, and after a dynamics-triggered message keep that rate for `N_GenCam` = 3 messages before relaxing [U, [ETSI EN 302 637-2](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/302600_302699/30263702/01.04.01_60/en_30263702v010401p.pdf)]. MinBand's θ_pos of 0.15 m is about 25× tighter than CAM's 4 m and 7× tighter than DIS's 1 m, which is right for camera-tracked entities at room scale and wrong at drone scale; the earlier report's point that these room constants must grow with geodetic error stands. What is new here is that MinBand has no per-entity minimum inter-delta interval at all: the trigger runs every tick at 30 Hz, and EVAL_FINDINGS #1 already shows velocity noise drives 75–90 % of updates and that 3 cm of position noise raises one walker from 118 to 168 B/s. A 100 ms floor per entity is a one-line change with a measurable effect.

### Repair, refresh and burst policy were settled by SCADA, codecs and games

The Tribes networking model is MinBand's architecture under another name: per-client ghosts with a dirty-bit mask, priority accumulated with time since last update, packets filled in priority order to a fixed budget, and loss repaired by OR-ing the lost dirty bits back so the *current* state is resent, with a stated design target of 28.8 kbit/s modems [U, [Tribes PDF](https://www.gamedevs.org/uploads/tribes-networking-model.pdf)]. Quake 3 deltas each snapshot against the last one the client acknowledged, keeps a 32-deep ring, and sends a full snapshot when the ack is too old [U]; Gaffer's snapshot-compression series reduced 900 cubes at 60 Hz from ~17 Mbit/s to ~256 kbit/s by quantising and delta-encoding against the last *acked* snapshot [U, [Gaffer](https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_compression/)]. The part MinBand lacks is priority: when the budget bites, the only knob is widening θ for everyone, whereas every game engine ranks objects and starves the least important. A spawn-first, class-change-second, position-error-third, refresh-last order is the earlier report's recommendation and the games' practice.

Industrial protocols add the ack-free pattern S1 needs. DNP3's event rule is `|new − old| > deadband` in unsigned arithmetic, events sit in preallocated per-type buffers that overflow when full, and unsolicited confirm and retry timeouts default to 5,000 ms, as implemented in opendnp3 bundled with pydnp3 0.1.0 ([pydnp3](https://pypi.org/project/pydnp3/0.1.0/)); OPC UA's `DeadbandType`, `DataChangeTrigger`, `QueueSize` and `DiscardOldest = True` are as implemented in asyncua 2.1.0 ([asyncua](https://pypi.org/project/asyncua/2.1.0/)), and discard-oldest is MinBand's "always send the latest state" in standard form. IEC 61850 GOOSE answers loss with no acknowledgements at all: on a change the publisher repeats the full state at increasing intervals up to a stable heartbeat, with `stNum` incremented per change and `sqNum` per repetition [U, unsourced this session; verify against IEC 61850-8-1 before quoting]. Deep space reached the same conclusion: Bundle Protocol v7 bundles carry a creation timestamp and a `lifetime` in milliseconds after which stale data is dropped rather than delivered late, as implemented in py-dtn7 0.3.0b1 ([py-dtn7](https://pypi.org/project/py-dtn7/0.3.0b1/)), and v7 removed custody transfer from the core protocol [U]. Market data separates an incremental channel from a snapshot channel for resync, and video transport lets the receiver ask for a keyframe (PLI/FIR) rather than retransmission [U, unsourced]. Together these say that S1's no-ack mode should not simply drop repair; it should repeat each delta once or twice at short intervals after the change and then fall back to the keyframe, which `tools/eval` can already score against the "repair off (keyframes only)" trace in the resilience chart.

The burst lesson comes from codecs. x264 bounds error propagation with a keyframe at most every 250 frames and spreads the cost with periodic intra refresh to avoid bitrate spikes, and IPTV learned to rate-limit synchronised keyframe requests after "I-frame storms" [U, unsourced]. MinBand's DESIGN §6 already records that a strict token-bucket rule "starves 160 B keyframes behind small deltas on a 2 kbps link"; on the Pi link box the `lora` and `telemetry` profiles use a 4-packet `netem` queue, so a multi-part keyframe burst that takes 0.6 s of link time will be dropped rather than queued. Pacing keyframe parts one per ack interval, or rolling the refresh across entities, keeps the floor bytes identical and removes the spike. This is the cross-domain finding closest to a P0 item.

### Crypto and integrity: the numbers to defend

The overhead numbers the team can say on stage are sourced. IEEE 802.15.4 security levels give 4-, 8- and 16-byte MICs with a 13-byte nonce built from the source address, a 4-byte frame counter and the security level ([IEEE P802.15 comments to NIST](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.15/dcn/24/15-24-0617-04-04ae-comments-to-nist-sp-800-232-ipd.docx)); an 8-byte tag plus the 4-byte counter is exactly 12 bytes on the wire. NIST SP 800-232 allows Ascon tags truncated to 32–128 bits but says tags under 64 bits "should only be chosen after a careful risk analysis", and the COSE-Ascon draft calls the 32-bit variant discouraged ([IETF draft](https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-ochkas-cose-ascon-03.xml)). MAVLink 2 signing costs a fixed 13 bytes and provides "authentication and integrity only" ([MAVLink](https://mavlink.io/en/guide/message_signing.html)), and its documented weakness is the one a hackathon will repeat: the 48-bit timestamp must increase monotonically, "it is the responsibility of each MAVLink system to store and restore the timestamp into persistent storage", and because vehicles are powered off by pulling the battery "the on-disk timestamp will typically remain at the value from the last key provisioning on reboot" ([PX4](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/mavlink/message_signing.html)). V2X accepted 70–220 bytes of signature and certificate on a ~200-byte CAM because unauthenticated safety messages were judged unusable [U]. A fielded mesh radio shows crypto has throughput cost too: Doodle Labs' miniOEM "using 256-bit AES caps throughput at 12 Mbps, while 128-bit AES gives full throughput" ([Doodle Labs datasheet](https://mouser.com/datasheet/2/895/miniOEM_2450-3446604.pdf)).

Integrity is not truth, and the precedents are explicit about it. ADS-B "does not use any authentication methods in its messages", ghost-aircraft injection and replay are "easy and practically feasible … with a SDR" ([arXiv 1904.09969](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.09969)), and the field's answer, because the format was frozen, was multilateration, radar fusion and motion plausibility rather than crypto [U]. The production decoder's version of that answer is concrete: pyModeS rejects a candidate position that implies more than 1,500 kt with a 2 km margin against a 5-position history, and still records the reject so a track anchored on phantoms can recover, as implemented in pyModeS 3.6.0 (`_pipe.py`) ([pyModeS](https://pypi.org/project/pyModeS/3.6.0/)). Wildlife ecology's Douglas filter does the same with maximum plausible speed and turning-angle tests [U]. For MinBand a receiver gate on per-class `max_speed` (3.0 m/s for a person in `core/src/classes.rs`) and on position jumps is two hours of work and answers both the replay objection and the mis-detection objection in part. The limit is also clear: consistency-based trust is itself attackable, because physical adversarial objects can make honest nodes look inconsistent ([TrustFlip](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2605.22122)), and self-reported positions cannot reveal a lying node without independent ranging ([arXiv 2312.03787](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2312.03787)). An inflatable tank produces an authentic, signed, correct-by-construction "tank" track; only pixels or a second sensor catch it.

### The reverse direction: what drone warfare has that the older fields lack

The notes are careful to say what runs the other way. None of the mature domains combines perceived, non-owned entities with confidence-weighted deltas; tactical data links share tracks with a quality field and animal tags share fixes with a location class, but neither runs the receiver's predictor on the sender, so MinBand's combination is still a gap rather than a reinvention. Three battlefield facts also break the older fields' assumptions. The link can be absent, not thin: DTN assumed scheduled or opportunistic contacts, Starlink's 2.5-hour outage left units flying "without a video feed" ([Polskie Radio](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/9766/Artykul/3556372,ukraine-says-starlink-outage-crippled-frontline-comms-for-25-hours)), and Russian UGV crews lost Starlink and were reduced to "handheld consoles from alongside" ([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/03/12/russias-ground-drones-need-infantry-escorts/)). The sender's own position is untrusted: AIS and ADS-B assume self-position is correct, while at the Ukrainian front "there is literally no GPS" ([Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/ukrainian-drone-pilots-often-weird-093202645.html)) and visual navigation vendors claim 10–20 m at best ([Vantor](https://vantor.com/blog/maxar-launches-raptor-a-first-of-its-kind-software-that-unlocks-next-gen-gps-resilience-for-autonomous-systems/); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/01/28/8018266)), so a track must carry its fix source and age, a field no older format has. And the receiver is adversarially saturated with decoys and false cues, which is why Ukraine's AI flags ~70 % of visible vehicles and a human reviews every cue ([Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/08/12/ukraine-ai-drone-target-detection/); [MWI](https://mwi.westpoint.edu/borrow-what-works-the-case-for-army-adoption-of-ukraines-combat-tested-horizontal-kill-web-architecture/)). The older fields did not need an evidence channel beside the state channel; drone warfare does.

ROK forces run Link-K, Link-16, Link-11 and KVMF under the Joint Tactical Data Link System ([KoreaScience](http://koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO202018355987856.page); [Hanwha Systems](https://www.hanwhasystems.com/en/business/defense/c5i/communication04.do)); KVMF is built on US VMF with MIL-STD-2045-47001D headers and more than 130 bit-packed messages, so compact tactical messaging is familiar to Korean judges, and the honest export story is KVMF or B2CS-style messages for the ROK Army, Link-K-like tracks for Navy and Air Force, and CoT for USFK and combined exercises; no evidence was found of ROK units using TAK. Crypto for government networks goes through the NIS-run KCMVP ([NIS](https://eng.nis.go.kr/EAF/1_7_2_1.do)), so S7 should be described as "a KCMVP-compatible boundary, key management out of scope". ## The red team's objections and what precedent answers

The strongest objections are not "detectors are bad". Taken in the order a panel asks them, with the precedent that answers each and the part no precedent answers.

**"The people who consume this must see pixels before acting."** PID under ROE, imagery-based collateral-damage estimation, Ukraine's man-in-the-loop policy and its video-proof kill bonus all make imagery the currency of engagement ([JAPCC](https://japcc.org/?p=11036); [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/04/30/politico-ukrainian-drone-operators-get-game-styled-points-for-russian-kills-exchange-them-for-more-weapons/)). Precedent answers the role, not the objection: JSTARS GMTI "can't identify a vehicle type or tell whether it is friendly, hostile, or a bystander" and survived as a cue for imagery ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_E-8_Joint_STARS)); Saildrone and the JKU SAR drone send state continuously and evidence rarely; Delta's published record pairs the cue with imagery. MinBand is a cueing and situational-awareness product, and S8 is what lets it be a traceable one. Nothing answers the accountability point: a track cannot generate the video proof the bonus system runs on.

**"Every fielded picture made of tracks produced a class of accident."** Vincennes, Patriot, BFT latency and Link 16 dual tracks are real ([Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2020/01/17/accidental-shootdown-iran-united-states-ukraine/); [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/?p=7463); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23516795/); [JHU APL](https://secwww.jhuapl.edu/techdigest/Content/techdigest/pdf/V23-N2-3/23-02-Bath.pdf)). The same fields produced the fixes: coasted-track bits and age displays, reporter election with a quality margin, hysteresis before merging. V2, S15 and S20 are those fixes in MinBand's code. The red team's warning that track-only engagements are accepted only for cooperative or physically characterised sources (IFF, radar signatures), never for a COCO classifier, stands; do not cite air defence as precedent for engagement on a MinBand track.

**"A smooth dead-reckoned icon hides staleness."** This is the FAA AD's exact language, and the answer is the same one aviation reached: the extrapolation must be visible as extrapolation, capped, and dropped on a timer. MinBand today starts the visible decay 6 s after the last update; S15 moves it to one missed keyframe, and S14 lets the ring be honest about the threshold in force. The DIS keyframe habit does not transfer unchanged: on a battlefield the absence of a heartbeat must change the display state, not just the error bound.

**"DARPA wants reduced video with traceability and object-agnostic selection, not class labels."** The DV019 topic asks for a 90 % reduction against full-frame mission video with preserved mission utility, 95–99 % objective, "traceable and object-agnostic selection of regions and events" and onboard-only execution ([Funding Landscape](https://fundinglandscape.com/opportunity/57d6e386-805c-4808-b122-d293cda973d7); [DARPA FAQ](https://www.darpa.mil/sites/default/files/attachment/2026-09/faq-dpa26bz05-dv019.pdf)). No cross-domain precedent rescues a fixed class list; the honest answer is that MinBand is the sync layer, the detector is replaceable, and the chip is the trace. Concede it and move on.

**"At your rate the honest competitor is a 100–200-byte thumbnail every few seconds, or compressed CoT."** True and sourced: MeshCore AI images are ~100–200 B ([Seeed](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/meshcore_ai_image_transmission_l2/)) and Meshtastic's TAKPacketV2 is a median 87 B ([TAKPacket SDK](https://klibs.io/project/meshtastic/TAKPacket-SDK)). The precedent that answers is Delta's own record: cue and evidence are complements, which is why S8 moves up and S23 adds the thumbnail as a baseline. The CoT objection is answered by degradation: Meshtastic drops when a message does not fit, MinBand widens thresholds; S6 measured against 87 B is the test. Only S13 answers the utility question, and only with a decoy condition, or the test "will be read as rigged".

**"Fixed thresholds introduce more inconsistency than they avoid, and widening under budget is where that bites."** The Salford DIS result is sourced ([Salford](https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1499305)). The precedents that answer are SAE J2945/1's trigger on receiver-side tracking error rather than sender dynamics [U], Diaz-Cacho's delta adapted to a target rate, and CAM's bounded min/max interval; S14 makes the inconsistency bound explicit on the wire, and S17 bounds the flapping. The `T_max` age cap that should bound absolute inconsistency never fires today because the 2 s keyframe resets ghosts first (EVAL_FINDINGS #2), which is fine at 2 s and not fine at 15 s (S19 must keep `t_max_ticks` below the stretched keyframe period).

**"Replay during a blackout is indistinguishable from state repair; the MAVLink timestamp trap; decoys produce authentic tracks."** 802.15.4-style counter plus 64-bit tag and a persisted high-water mark answer replay (S7); the pyModeS plausibility gate answers crude injection (S22); nothing answers decoys except pixels and a second sensor, and the notes found no measured false-identification rate against decoys anywhere, so the only honest number is a confusion matrix on the team's own data.

**"Silence can be jammed selectively, turning 'entity stopped' into 'entity continues'."** Finding 1 is the answer: silence is information only while loss is excluded, so a missed keyframe must be a displayed event (S15), and the ground side's own ack recency is the receiver's evidence that the channel is live.

**"Stock Meshtastic at 5–20 B/s is below your 60 B/s floor; 'radio-neutral' needs a per-radio table."** Correct. The table is: ELRS telemetry ~10 B/s at defaults, Meshtastic LongFast tens of B/s and airtime-limited, SiK/RFD-class 64 kbit/s air rate ([jDrones](https://wiki.jdrones.com/com/jdrf900x)), HF 3.2–9.6 kbit/s, Iridium SBD 340 B per message, MANET tens of Mbit/s where video returns. MinBand fits the middle three today and the first two only after S6 and S19.

**"Operators adopt automation only when the alternative is nothing."** First-generation AI guidance in Ukraine was used "only … when jamming made it impossible to do the job manually" ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2026/01/02/ukraines-killer-ai-drones-are-back-with-a-vengeance/)). This is not an objection to answer but the pitch to adopt: blackout survival, not video replacement.

## Conclusion

The research changes the project's self-description more than its code. MinBand has been described as dead reckoning plus a budget controller; the precedents say it is a Tribes-style ghost manager carrying DNP3-style report-by-exception with a DTN-style no-retransmission rule, and that the one thing every mature version of that pattern added which MinBand still lacks is a self-declared error bar on each message and a visible coasting state when the heartbeat stops. Those two additions cost an afternoon, turn V2 from decoration into the demo's strongest claim, and give the CoT exporter a `ce` that a ROK or NATO consumer can actually use. The airtime finding reframes the headline: on the radios drones carry, the question is not how many bytes a feed costs but what fraction of a shared channel it occupies, and MinBand's answer is defensible on HF and SiK-class links today and on LoRa and ELRS only after the compact tier lands.

The deeper implication for after Seoul is that the evidence channel and the state channel are different products that the older fields never had to separate and drone warfare cannot combine. The twin carries the cue with an honest age and error; the chip carries the evidence a human must see; the budget decides how often each gets through. Every fielded precedent that reached a decision-maker, from the filter room to Delta, paired the two. Building MinBand as the state channel alone was the right way to win a hackathon; building it as the layer that paces both is the way to be kept after testing.
