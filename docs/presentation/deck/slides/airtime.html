On shared radios the budget is airtime, not bytes. One walker takes 7.8 % of HF and 56 % of a 2 kbit/s LoRa channel (Meshtastic MediumSlow); a 16 B packet costs 354 ms on LongFast.

e2e/test/drones.test.ts: 8 sim edges, server splits the 8 kbit/s budget, hf profile (500 ms, 1 % loss). Airtime: serial model for HF, Semtech formula for LoRa.

Say "N feeds fit in X percent of the channel", not "N feeds fit in X bytes". The acks matter too: today the ground station transmits about as often as the drones, which is why quiet simplex mode is the first next step.
