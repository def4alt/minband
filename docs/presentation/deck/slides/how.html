Golden vectors pass native x86-64, WASM in Node, and aarch64 Linux (qemu); the same bytes and the same extrapolations.

The link box shapes in the kernel, outside our server: the demo does not grade its own homework.

Point at the hardware on the table: phone, Pi, laptop. The Pi is the radio: it reproduces rate, delay and loss, not framing. The predictor is bit-identical across platforms; golden vectors prove it, including on aarch64.
