What we do not claim

It is a link layer, not a drone and not a detector.

Not new math

The delta idea is DIS dead reckoning. The engineering is in the honesty: error bars, coasting, cadence.

Not the detector

Edge detection is crowded. A COCO "truck" is not a BMP; decoys defeat low-resolution labels.

Not stealth

Fewer transmissions mean less airtime, not undetectable. Today the ground station acks about as often as the drone sends.

Not unbounded savings

Manoeuvring targets and decoys push the rate toward the keyframe floor. The controller caps it; fidelity pays.

This slide earns trust with military judges. Every point here is something a judge would otherwise raise. The stealth point leads into the first next step: a quiet, simplex mode.
